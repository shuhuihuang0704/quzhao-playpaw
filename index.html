关于我们About us

趣爪创新宠物用品有限公司 Playpaw Pet Products Co., Ltd.

趣是一起玩，爪是它踩过地板的声音。智创是先把玩法想明白，再用羊毛、棉绳和陶做出来。对外英文名是 Playpaw，外国人一眼能读。

Qu is play; zhao is the paw. Inventive play means we design the game first, then make it. Overseas, the name is Playpaw — easy to read, easy to say.

羊毛毡在浙江，陶碗在景德镇附近，木头在东阳车。每批不多，卖完再做，不赶。

Felt in Zhejiang, bowls near Jingdezhen, wood turned in Dongyang. Small batches. When one run is gone, we make the next — no rush.

配送：中国大陆满 150 元起送。商品合计未满 299 元加收 8 元配送费，满 299 元免配送费。海外按实际计。七日内未使用可退。已经玩过的，就留给它吧。

Shipping: mainland orders start from ¥150. Under ¥299, a ¥8 delivery fee applies; ¥299 and above ships free. International shipping calculated separately. Unused returns within seven days. If they’ve already played with it, keep it.

材料Materials

羊毛、亚麻、棉、木头和陶，摸得到，也放心咬。没有会响的塑料芯片。

Wool, linen, cotton, wood, and clay — nice to touch, fine to chew. No sound chips.

节奏Pace

小炉小批就能做，先让它玩起来再加量。卖完了等下一炉，不赶着铺货。

Small kiln batches: start small, then make more. When it’s gone, we wait — no rush to flood the shelf.

使用Use

请陪在旁边玩。细小零件不适合咬得很凶的大型犬。

Stay close while they play. Small parts are not for heavy chewers.
